# Business Problem

AtliQ Hardware needs a reliable way to monitor actual sales quantity, forecast quantity, product performance, market trends, and customer/channel performance.

The original BI360 dashboard supports business reporting, but a complete analytics workflow also requires a reliable data layer before reporting. This includes raw storage, metadata management, validation checks, transformation logic, curated outputs, and warehouse-ready tables.

## Analytical Questions

The project focuses on answering questions such as:

- Which markets have the highest sold quantity?
- Which products have the largest forecast gaps?
- Which customer/channel combinations drive the highest sales volume?
- How accurate are forecasts compared with actual sold quantity?
- Which reporting dates show the highest overall forecast error?

## Business Value

The workflow improves trust in reporting by validating the data before it reaches dashboards and by separating raw, curated, and reporting layers.
